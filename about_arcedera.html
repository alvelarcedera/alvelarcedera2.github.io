About Me

Personal Background

Hi! I am Jhon Alvel Arcedera, a sophomore student studying Bachelor of Multimedia Arts whose fascination is on exploring various avenues to communicate through visual and audio mediums. Some of my passions are film production, music video production, music composition and production, and digital storytelling, where I can integrate my passion for music with my creativity. Through my academic life and personal work, I strive to improve my technical skills and hone my unique creative talent while at the same time learning how to tell stories that move people.

Skills

Career Goals

I have a desire to be a producer and cinematographer in the music industry where I can combine my love of music and interest in filmmaking.
